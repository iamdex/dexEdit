# dexEdit

A fast place to put notes.

Every note is a plain .md file sitting in a folder you chose. Open it with anything else. Move it anywhere. Delete the app tomorrow and every note you wrote is still exactly where you left it.

Mac, iPhone and iPad. No account, no newsletter — one email, once.

Thank you. One email, when there is something to download.

## Files, not a database

Most notes apps keep your writing in a shape only they can read, then offer you an export button as consolation. dexEdit has exactly one format: markdown text, in memory and on disk, with no conversion step anywhere between them.

So styled and raw are not two documents. They are two ways of drawing the same characters — the markers stay in the text and get out of your way, rather than disappearing behind a rendering. That is what the button at the top of this page is doing to this page.

## Open before you've finished thinking

Put New Note in Control Centre, on the Lock Screen, or on the iPhone's Action button. One press and there is a cursor waiting — no home screen, no app icon to find, no empty state to dismiss first.

There is no save button either. You stop typing, it's written. The file names itself after your first heading and renames itself when you change your mind.

## iCloud, honestly

Keep the folder in iCloud Drive and the Mac, the iPhone and the iPad read the very same files. Not a copy, not a sync service of ours in the middle — there is no server here to hold your notes on.

  • - A note that hasn't come down to this device yet stays in the list, marked as waiting. It never quietly vanishes.
  • - An edit made on another device turns up while you're looking at the list, without touching anything.
  • - Write on two devices while one is offline and you keep both versions, side by side. Nothing is silently chosen for you.

## What it will never do

accounts
tracking, analytics, telemetry
a server that holds your notes
plugins
themes
AI anything
tags, backlinks, graph view

This list is the feature. Every line on it is a thing that would have made the app slower to open, harder to explain, or a worse place to leave something you might want in ten years.

## It isn't out yet

It is built and it is in daily use, on all three. What is left is the part Apple does. Leave an address and you'll hear once, on the day you can actually download it.

No newsletter. No “we've updated our privacy policy”. One email.
